Android

  1. Tap Download for Android, then open WitchcraftCompanion.apk from your downloads.
  2. If Android asks, allow your browser or Files app to install unknown apps, then tap Install.
  3. Updates: the app checks this page once a day (or tap Settings → App Updates → Check for updates). Tap Update now and confirm on Android's screen. The first time, Android asks you to let the Companion install updates.

iPhone & iPad

  1. Open this page in Safari and tap Open on iPhone & web.
  2. Tap the Share button (the square with an arrow), then Add to Home Screen, then Add.
  3. Open it from the new icon. It downloads everything once and then works offline.
  4. Updates arrive by themselves whenever you open it while online.

Your entries are stored on your device. Use Settings → Export backup now and then to keep a copy safe.

What's new in 4.1
